Quick Scrambled Eggs

Heat 1 tablespoon of butter in a non-stick pan over medium heat. 
Crack 3 eggs into a bowl and whisk with 2 tablespoons of milk.
Pour the egg mixture into the hot pan.
Let it sit for 20 seconds, then gently stir with a spatula.
Continue cooking for 1-2 minutes, stirring occasionally.
Season with salt and pepper to taste.
Remove from heat and serve immediately.
