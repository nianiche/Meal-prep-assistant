Chicken Tikka Masala

Ingredients:
- 2 lbs chicken breast, cut into chunks
- 1 large onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- Basmati rice for serving

Instructions:
1. Heat oil in large pan over medium-high heat
2. Add chicken and cook until browned, about 6-8 minutes
3. Add onion and cook until softened, 5 minutes
4. Add garlic, ginger, and all spices. Cook 1 minute
5. Add tomatoes and simmer 10 minutes
6. Stir in cream and cilantro
7. Serve over rice
