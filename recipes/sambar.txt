1. oil roast 6 red chillies, 1/2 coconut, 1/2 tspn tamarind. Then grind to paste.
2. Boil hard veggies like drumstick (About 10)
3. Pressure cook about 3/4 + cup of toor dal
4. When dal is done, add hard veggies, soft veggies, 1/2 tomato. Steam for 5 mins.
5. Later add ground paste, 2 tbsp sambar, 1/2 tspn salt. Saute for 10 mins
5. Oil, add mustard seeds, 1/2 onion julienne. Once onions brown, add them to dal
