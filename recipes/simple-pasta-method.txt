Simple Pasta

Boil 8 ounces of spaghetti in salted water for 10 minutes until al dente.
Meanwhile, heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a large pan.
Add 3 cloves garlic, minced, and sauté for 30 seconds.
Add 1 can crushed tomatoes and simmer for 5 minutes.
Season with 1 teaspoon dried basil, salt and pepper.
Drain the pasta and toss with the sauce.
Sprinkle with 1/2 cup parmesan cheese before serving.
